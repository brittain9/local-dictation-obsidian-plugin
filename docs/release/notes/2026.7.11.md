## Highlights

- **Read notes aloud entirely on-device.** Pocket TTS adds natural local speech
  in English, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, and Italian, with selectable
  voices, pause/resume/stop commands, and pitch-preserving 0.75–2× speed control.

## Improvements

- **Task-aware model management.** Dictation and read-aloud models now have
  separate selections and browsing views. Optional voices appear immediately
  after installation without restarting Obsidian.
- **Markdown-aware playback.** Read aloud keeps headings, links, lists, and table
  text while skipping frontmatter, code, and math.
