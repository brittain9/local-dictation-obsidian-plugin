## Highlights

- **Recover dictation you thought was lost.** New commands reinsert the most recent finalized utterance at the cursor and restore the original raw transcript after an LLM transform rewrites it — no more losing a sentence to an overeager cleanup.
- **Timestamps are now simple and work the same with every model.** Frequency is a fixed choice — at intervals, every phrase, or at Smart paragraph breaks — no matter which model is active. With Whisper, "Every phrase" places sentence-level landmarks inside long stretches of speech. The word-by-word inline timestamp mode is gone, and switching models can no longer strand an unavailable setting.
- **Cap automatic speaker labels.** A new Maximum speakers setting (Automatic or 1–8) stops diarization from inventing extra speakers; later turns are assigned to the nearest existing speaker.

## Improvements

- **Settings apply as you change them.** The timestamp, smart paragraph, speaker label, and LLM transform modals now auto-save every change and offer a single Reset — no more Save buttons or losing edits by closing a modal.
- **A cleaner dictation sidebar.** Transform controls are grouped by what they own, presets stay readable in narrow panes, advanced transcript options moved into focused modals, and "Speaking style" is now "Phrase finalization" with the speed-versus-pause trade-off explained inline.
- **Better paragraph rhythm out of the box.** Smart paragraph defaults now distinguish a line break (4 s pause) from a new paragraph (10 s), so ordinary thinking pauses no longer split your note into fragments.

## Fixes

- **Microphone hiccups no longer break a session.** Dictation stops cleanly when the microphone disconnects, and a saved device that has gone missing is cleared after falling back instead of failing on every start.
- **Live models keep more of what you said.** Moonshine final decodes can reach the true end of an utterance instead of truncating, and voice detection preserves complete speech onsets so first syllables are no longer clipped.

## Compatibility

- **Clearer platform and language expectations.** The Linux support boundary is now documented with a troubleshooting guide, and language support states plainly what is English-only today and what the multilingual roadmap covers.

## Internal

- **Release pipeline correctness hardening.** The release workflow's metadata gate and native build legs gained integration coverage, catching version drift and packaging mistakes before they can ship.
