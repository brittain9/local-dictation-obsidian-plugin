## Highlights

- **Dictate in eight languages.** Whisper Large V3 Turbo and experimental Nemotron 3.5 ASR now support English, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, and Japanese, with either a manual language choice or automatic detection.
- **Try NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 ASR for live multilingual text.** The new experimental streaming engine adds a multilingual alternative for provisional text while you speak; Moonshine remains the recommended English live-dictation default.

## Improvements

- **Language choices match the exact model.** Settings only offer languages and automatic detection when the selected model's installed weights support them, while Moonshine, Cohere Transcribe, and `.en` Whisper models stay clearly English-only.

## Known Limitations

- **Automatic detection chooses one language per utterance.** Manual selection remains the most predictable option, and mixed-language code-switching within one utterance is not yet a quality guarantee.

## Internal

- **Multilingual quality is now a release gate.** Pinned human-speech fixtures exercise the real Whisper and Nemotron product paths across all eight supported languages before publication.
