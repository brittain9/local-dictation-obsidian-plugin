## Features

- **Use Local Dictation in your language.** The complete plugin interface now follows Obsidian's
  language in English, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, and Japanese. This
  includes setup, settings, commands, model management, notices, and optional LLM transforms.
  Regional variants such as `pt-BR` use their matching base-language translation, while other
  languages continue to receive the full English interface.
