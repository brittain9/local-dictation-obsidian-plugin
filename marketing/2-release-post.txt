------------------------------------------------------------

**Local Dictation** is a free, open-source plugin that turns your voice into clean notes in Obsidian — and it runs entirely on your machine. No cloud, no account, no telemetry.

The big update: dictation no longer just drops raw text into your note. A **local LLM cleans it up for you** — fixing filler, false starts, punctuation, and capitalization — or rewrites/summarizes/formats it however you want. All on-device, through Ollama.

**🎙️ Transcription that's actually good**
- **Cohere Transcribe** — tops the Hugging Face Open ASR Leaderboard, running locally.
- **Whisper** — mature offline transcription, multiple size/speed options.
- **Silero v6 VAD** for clean, real-time speech boundaries.
- **Hardware accelerated** — Metal on macOS, CUDA on Windows/Linux (CPU works everywhere).

**🧠 New: local LLM transformation**
Pick a style and your dictation comes out finished, not raw. Built-in presets:
- **Clean up** — kill filler and fix punctuation, keep your voice
- **Professional writing** — tighten into polished prose
- **TLDR** — summary on top, cleaned transcript below
- **Markdown formatting** — headings, lists, emphasis, code blocks
- **Brain dump organizer** — cluster a ramble into themes, action items, and decisions
- **Voice commands** *(experimental)* — say "make this a list" or "summarize the above" and it does it

Run cleanup **after every phrase** or **once when you stop**. Save your own custom presets too.

**🔀 Local by default, your call on the cloud**
- **Local / Remote / Auto** routing: keep everything on-device, or let **Auto** send only the oversized jobs (the ones that crawl on a local model) to OpenRouter.
- **Searchable model picker** with restaurant-style price tiers (Free / $–$$$$) so you see cost before you pick.
- **Remote kill switch** — one toggle forces everything local, instantly.

**🔒 Private by design**
Your audio never leaves your device — transcription is always local. The only thing that can ever go to the cloud is the optional OpenRouter transform, and only if *you* turn it on.

**📦 Get it**
- Install from Obsidian's **Community Plugins** → search **"Local Dictation"**
- A first-run setup wizard downloads the engine and a model for you
- Desktop only (macOS, Windows, Linux) • MIT licensed • free & open source

GitHub: https://github.com/brittain9/local-dictation-obsidian-plugin

Feedback and feature requests welcome — would love to hear how you'd use it. 🙏
